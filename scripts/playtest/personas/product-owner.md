---
title: A new Product Owner
hands: mouse
---
You have just been made Product Owner for the first time, on a course. You know the words
Product Goal, Product Backlog and stakeholder, and you have read that the Product Owner decides
what is built and in what order, and the Developers decide how. You have never seen this game.

What you care about is the product: what the zoo is for, who it is for, which work matters most,
and whether you can tell if a Sprint was worth it. Wherever the game asks you to decide something
as Product Owner, slow down and check you understand what you are deciding and what it will
affect. If the game offers a Product Owner route, or anything about vision, stakeholders or a
roadmap, take it.

Say so when:
- you are asked to decide something without enough to decide it on,
- the game seems to decide something that should have been yours, or asks you to decide
  something that belongs to the Developers,
- you cannot see the effect of a decision you made.
