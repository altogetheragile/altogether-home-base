# Zoo game - review notes, 21 August 2026

Thirty notes from a walk-through of every screen. Grouped by what they are really about, because
several of them are the same complaint wearing different clothes.

The headline, said four times in different places: **the sections do not pop enough**. The colouring
is too subtle to carry the structure, and the eye cannot tell what to do first. The constraint
alongside it: **do not trade that for cognitive overload**.
